# CS3 Case Study: Chicago Rent Analysis

## Case Study Question
Do business openings appear to be associated with rent growth in Chicago ZIP codes?

## Context
This case study is designed for 2nd-year UVA data science students. It uses real-world data from Chicago business licenses and Zillow rent data to explore neighborhood change.

## Student Task
Students will:
- Reproduce the dataset using the scripts
- Generate visualizations
- Interpret patterns between business openings and rent growth
- Discuss limitations of the analysis

## Final Deliverable
A memo or slide deck explaining whether business openings are associated with rent growth and what limitations exist.
